Welcome to jBlueprint!

I'm Jorge Becerro, a software architect and distributed systems enthusiast. I created this space to share practical notes on architecture, design patterns, and the disciplines that define quality software engineering, with a primary focus on the backend ecosystem.

Beyond theory, we explore new technologies and frameworks that bring real value when planning and building projects — whether large professional challenges or personal initiatives. My goal is for this blog to serve as a useful guide for building software that is robust, scalable, and above all, clear.
